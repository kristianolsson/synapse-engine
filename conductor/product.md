# Product Definition - Synapse Engine

## Vision
The Synapse Engine is a personal, "dumb pipe" infrastructure designed for frictionless life management. It serves as a bridge between common communication channels (Email, Telegram) and a centralized, LLM-powered "brain" (the Gemini CLI/Synapse Vault). The engine ensures that every thought, task, and piece of information is captured, organized, and retrieved with minimal effort.

## Target User
- **Individual Power User:** Specifically designed for personal life management, focusing on high-signal capture and automated organization for a single user.

## Core Goals
- **Frictionless Capture:** Record thoughts or tasks from anywhere (Email, Telegram) without the need for specialized apps.
- **Centralized Intelligence:** Create a single, intelligent repository that remembers and organizes all ingested data.
- **Automated Follow-ups:** Proactively manage pending items and ensure no task is forgotten through system-driven reminders.

## Key Features
- **Intelligent Task Capture:** Automatically parses incoming messages into structured tasks or notes within the vault.
- **Contextual Reminders:** Uses the ingestion pipe to send proactive updates or follow-ups based on vault data.
- **Multi-format Ingestion:** Supports text, voice notes, images, and documents to provide a comprehensive capture experience.

## User Experience (UX)
- **Minimalist & Functional:** Prioritizes a clean, command-line-like interface for interactions, providing concise confirmations and clear error messages to maintain speed and focus.
